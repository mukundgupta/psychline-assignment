# Technology Stack

The entire site is coded in HTML/CSS/JS utilizing some JS libraries for effects.

# About Project

Link to published site: https://mukundgupta.github.io/WebPortfolio/

This was made and updated over the course of last year and finalized for my college applications. Some parts of it in the portfolio section are still to be updated.
I have used a lot of effects to represent what I like, including glassmorphism in the menu and about sections, as well as interactive blob-like backgrounds. It also has a feedback page which is connected to formspree which sends the feedback to my inbox.
