# About Me

I've always been interested in design and how things work - from making posters and brochures to creating
entire animations and websites. I'm also interested in how tech blends with design. For me, programming is yet
another medium for expressing my ideas.
Over the years I have dabbled in graphic design, game development, full-stack programming, animation, vfx,
music, as well as hardware and electronics using the Raspberry Pi. I love design and technology, playing with visuals, and crafting interactive experiences.
